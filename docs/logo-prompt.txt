Tool: built-in image_gen (transparent PNG; no CLI/API fallback).

New logo, no wordmark and no text. Form a distinctive rounded angular ribbon: a right-facing play triangle integrated with a downward motion into a compact U-shaped save tray; the whole silhouette should feel like a flowing capital A without literal lettering. Extremely simple, bold, balanced, recognizable at 32 pixels. Use only deep teal #006B5E and mint #8DD9CC, with deep teal dominant.
